The idea

One thruster, one way out

Tap to launch

Every pod points one way. Tap it and it fires off along that line, as long as nothing sits in front of it.

Blocked? Bump

A pod side-on turns to face the bump. A row of pods in line passes it along and knocks the last one out. Nose to nose, the bump fades.

Colour is the axis

Crimson, citron and ultramarine mark the three axes. When a pod turns, its stripe changes colour, so you see the new heading at once.

Why it stays calm

A puzzle you read, not guess

See it before it moves

A pod's path flashes before it goes: bright means clear, striped means it will bump. You learn the board instead of trial and error.

120 berths, six twists

Each chapter adds one idea and teaches it in a single berth, from pods passing a bump along to anchored and twin pods.

  • First Berth
  • Cradle Lines
  • Nose to Nose
  • Anchored
  • Twin Pods
  • Full Dock

No lives, no timers

Each berth gives you a fuel budget. Run dry and you try again at once, nothing lost. Undo, Beacon and Fuel cell help when you want them.

How to play

Three moves to learn

  1. Drag to orbit the berth.Turn the cluster to see every pod and where it points.
  2. Tap a pod.It launches if its way is clear, or bumps the first pod in its path and turns it.
  3. Clear every pod before the fuel runs out.Each tap uses one fuel. Fewer taps earn more stars.
Questions

Before you dock

Is Spinberth free?

Yes. All 120 berths are free to play. Optional booster packs cost $0.99 to $4.99. There is no subscription and nothing to unlock.

Are there ads?

No. The game has no ads, no ad networks, no analytics and no account. Read the privacy policy.

What happens when I run out of fuel?

You try the berth again straight away and lose nothing. If you have them, a Fuel cell adds three fuel or Undo takes back your last tap.

When and where can I play?

Spinberth is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. It plays offline.
