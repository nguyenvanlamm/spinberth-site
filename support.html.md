# Spinberth Support

Bump a pod. Watch it turn. Clear the berth.

## Contact

## How to play

Drag to turn the berth. Tap a pod to launch it along the way it points. If another pod is in the way, your pod bumps
it: a pod side-on turns to face the bump, a pod in line passes the bump along and the last one is knocked out, and two
pods nose to nose cancel the bump. Each tap uses one fuel; clear every pod before the fuel runs out. Running out costs
nothing: try again at once. The rules are also in the game under Pause → ?.

## Purchases

All 120 berths are free. The Supply depot sells optional booster packs (Undo Pack, Beacon Pack, Fuel Pack and Dock
Crate); each is used up as you play. Boosters you bought stay in your stock on the device that bought them until you
use them; they do not move to another device and are removed if you delete the app. A pack that was paid for but did
not arrive is delivered the next time you open the game. Settings → Restore purchases asks the App Store again;
because every pack is consumable, it usually finds nothing to restore.

## Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
