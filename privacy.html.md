# Spinberth Privacy Policy

Spinberth · last updated November 21, 2026

Spinberth is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

## What stays on your device

- Your progress: the berths you cleared and their stars, your boosters (Undo, Beacon, Fuel cell), which booster gifts
  you already received, and which tips you have seen.
- Your settings: Sound on or off.
- Which purchases of the Undo Pack, Beacon Pack, Fuel Pack and Dock Crate were already delivered (the store's
  transaction numbers, so a pack is never granted twice).

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

## Purchases

All 120 berths are free. Booster packs are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive
no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

## Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
