# Spinberth

Bump a pod. Watch it turn. Clear the berth.

Spinberth is a 3D puzzle on a zero-gravity cargo dock for iPhone. A blocked tap is a move, not a mistake: the pod you
bump turns a quarter-turn and opens a new way out. Coming soon to the App Store.

At a glance: 120 berths, all free · No ads · No account · No tracking.

## One thruster, one way out

- **Tap to launch.** Every pod points one way. Tap it and it fires off along that line, as long as nothing sits in front of it.
- **Blocked? Bump.** A pod side-on turns to face the bump. A row of pods in line passes it along and knocks the last one out. Nose to nose, the bump fades.
- **Colour is the axis.** Crimson, citron and ultramarine mark the three axes. When a pod turns, its stripe changes colour, so you see the new heading at once.

## A puzzle you read, not guess

- **See it before it moves.** A pod's path flashes before it goes: bright means clear, striped means it will bump.
- **120 berths, six twists.** Chapters: First Berth, Cradle Lines, Nose to Nose, Anchored, Twin Pods, Full Dock.
- **No lives, no timers.** Each berth gives you a fuel budget. Run dry and you try again at once, nothing lost. Undo, Beacon and Fuel cell help when you want them.

## How to play

1. **Drag to orbit the berth.** Turn the cluster to see every pod and where it points.
2. **Tap a pod.** It launches if its way is clear, or bumps the first pod in its path and turns it.
3. **Clear every pod before the fuel runs out.** Each tap uses one fuel. Fewer taps earn more stars.

## Questions

- **Is Spinberth free?** Yes. All 120 berths are free to play. Optional booster packs cost $0.99 to $4.99. There is no subscription and nothing to unlock.
- **Are there ads?** No. The game has no ads, no ad networks, no analytics and no account.
- **What happens when I run out of fuel?** You try the berth again straight away and lose nothing. If you have them, a Fuel cell adds three fuel or Undo takes back your last tap.
- **When and where can I play?** Spinberth is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. It plays offline.
